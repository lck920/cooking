## Ingredients
### Fine Grind Ingredients
- 8 shallots, sliced
- 6 garlic cloves, sliced
- 3 lemongrass, sliced
- 3 inch ginger, sliced
- 3 inch galangal, sliced
- 3 pcs fresh turmeric, sliced
- 3 red chilies, sliced
- 3 cili padi, sliced
- 4 candlenuts
- 1 big red onion, sliced

### Chili Paste
- 30g dried chili, cut into slices
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- Salt to taste

### Other Ingredients
- Turmeric leaves, sliced
- 4 pcs lime leaves
- Chicken
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 4 tbsp kerisik
- 2-3 pcs asam gelugur (can be substituted with tamarind paste)

## Steps
1. Blend the shallots, garlic, lemongrass, ginger, galangal, fresh turmeric, chilies, candlenuts, and red onion with some water.
2. Blend the dried chili, garlic, and salt with some water.
3. Heat up oil in a pan and sauté the rendang paste until it becomes darker.
4. When it's cooked, add in the chili paste and lime leaves, and stir.
5. Add in the chicken and stir until it is cooked and the sauce thickens.
6. Add in the coconut milk, asam gelugur, and kerisik, and stir.
7. Add in the turmeric leaves, salt, and sugar to taste.
