## Ingredients
- 120g unsalted butter
- 150g brown sugar
- 30g caster sugar
- 1 L-sized egg or 2 M-sized eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla essence
- 175g plain flour
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp fine salt
- 100g chocolate coins or chocolate bar, chopped
- 50g chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat, stirring often, until it turns golden brown and smells nutty.
2. Pour the browned butter, including the brown bits, into a large bowl and let it cool for 5 minutes.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the plain flour, baking soda and salt until evenly mixed.
4. Add the brown sugar and caster sugar to the warm butter and whisk until combined.
5. Add the egg and whisk well.
6. Add the vanilla essence and whisk well.
7. Add half of the dry ingredients and fold in with a spatula, then add the rest. Mix until just combined.
8. Fold in half of the chocolate. Set the other half aside for topping.
9. Cover and chill the dough in the fridge for 12–24 hours.
10. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
11. Line a baking tray with parchment paper. A little butter under the paper helps it stay in place.
12. Scoop the dough onto the tray with an ice cream scoop, leaving space between each cookie.
13. Press the remaining chocolate on top of each dough ball.
14. Bake for about 10 minutes, until golden brown on the edges but still slightly underdone in the center.
15. Let the cookies cool on the tray for 5–10 minutes before moving them.

## Tips
1. Do not overmix after adding the flour. Overmixing develops gluten and makes the cookies tough.
2. Chilling the dough deepens the flavour and stops the cookies from spreading too much.
3. The cookies keep cooking on the tray after coming out of the oven, so take them out while the center still looks soft.

## Notes
- Use half the brown sugar for less-sweet cookies.
