## Ingredients
- 2 chicken thighs and drumsticks
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 potatoes
- 1 carrot, peeled
- 1 green cabbage
- A bunch of green onions
- 6 cloves of garlic
- Some ginger
- White rice
- 1/2 bowl of soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- 2 tbsp brown sugar

## Steps
### Preps
1. Cook the rice in a rice cooker.
2. Debone the chicken, leaving the drumsticks for the soup and separating the thighs from the bones.
3. Roll-cut the carrot.
4. Cut the potatoes into smaller pieces.
5. Dice half of the onion into smaller pieces.
6. Finely shred a quarter of the cabbage.
7. Slice up the rest of the green onion for garnish.

### Soup
1. Put the diced onion and some salt into a hot oiled pan.
2. Put the shredded cabbage into the pan once the onions are fragrant.
3. Add in the chicken bones and drumsticks, a few cloves of garlic and ginger, green onion bottoms, carrot, and potato.
4. Cover the pot with water and let it simmer away until it's fully cooked.

### Chicken
1. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper.
2. Lay the chicken thighs skin-side down in a medium-heat pan.
3. Make the chicken marinade with soy sauce, brown sugar, minced garlic, sliced ginger, and green onion bottoms.
4. Cut the rest of the cabbage into wedges.
5. Once cooked, set the chicken thighs aside and reserve the rendered fat.
6. Sear the cabbage wedges in the pan.
7. Add a splash of water to release the fond, add the chicken marinade, and let the cabbage cook until the sauce thickens.

### Fried Rice
1. Chop up the rest of the cabbage and cook it with the chicken fat in a new pan.
2. Cut the rest of the chicken into slices and sear it in a hot pan.
3. Set aside the cooked chicken slices.
4. In the same pan, add in the green onion bottoms, onion, rice, a touch of soy sauce, and sesame oil, then add back the chicken and cabbage.
5. Serve.

## Tips
1. Roll-cut the carrot for even-sized pieces for the soup.
