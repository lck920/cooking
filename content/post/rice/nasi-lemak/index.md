## Ingredients
### Nasi
- 3 shallots, sliced
- 2 inch ginger, sliced
- 1 lemongrass, smashed
- 3 pandan leaves, tied in a knot
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 star anise
- 2 cups basmati rice
- Coconut milk, 1:1.5 ratio to rice
- 2 tsp salt

### Sambal
- 8 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 inch ginger, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced (for sautéing)
- 7-8 red onions, sliced (for blending)
- 30g anchovies, washed (for blending)
- Belacan
- Tamarind pulp, mixed with water
- 30g dried chili (spicy), cut into small pieces
- 200g dried fat chili (non-spicy)
- 1-2 pandan leaves, tied in a knot

### Condiments
- Groundnuts
- Anchovies, washed
- Boiled eggs, peeled
- Cucumber, sliced

## Steps
### Nasi
1. Heat up some oil in the rice cooker.
2. Add in the sliced shallots, sliced ginger, lemongrass, pandan leaves, cinnamon stick, and star anise.
3. Put in the rice and stir.
4. Add in 1.5 cups of coconut milk per 1 cup of rice.
5. Add in the salt.
6. Cook the rice.

### Sambal
1. Boil the dried chili (spicy) in hot water.
2. Cut and boil the dried fat chili (non-spicy), then soak overnight.
3. Blend the garlic, ginger, and red onions with some water.
4. Blend the anchovies and red onions with some water.
5. Blend the dried chili and dried fat chili with oil.
6. Heat up oil in a pan, then sauté the red onion until golden brown.
7. Put in the pandan leaves and fry until fragrant.
8. Add in the onion paste and slow cook until it becomes dry.
9. Add in the chili paste and slow cook until cooked.
10. Add in 3-4 cubes of gula melaka and 1-2 tbsp of white sugar (optional).
11. Add the tamarind juice so the colour becomes darker.
12. Add salt to taste.
13. Continue cooking on low heat, stirring constantly until done.
14. On low heat, add in the sliced red onion and stir until cooked.

### Condiments
1. Fry the groundnuts and anchovies in hot oil until done.

## Tips
1. Do not fry the groundnuts for too long, as they can become bitter and burnt.
